Head spa and scalp care
in one place

We offer both head spa and scalp care: a 20–30 minute membership course, head spa (20–30 or 50–60 minutes) and head spa + basic treatment.

Hair care options include keratin treatment, repair straightening treatment and 6-session TOKIO and Bunyadi treatments. Hayun completed the RENE FURTERER Tricologist course. For cut + head spa packages, see Haircuts.

Recommended for

  • Anyone looking for head spa and scalp care
  • Those who want keratin or repair treatments after a perm or color
  • Anyone wanting steady care over a 6-session course
  • Those who want a head spa on a weekday evening

FAQ

Treatment FAQ

How much is a head spa and how long does it take?
Head spa (scalp care) is ₩50,000 for 20–30 minutes or ₩80,000 for 50–60 minutes. The membership course (scalp care) is ₩30,000 for 20–30 minutes, and head spa + basic treatment is ₩130,000.
What hair treatments do you offer?
Keratin treatment ₩200,000–320,000, repair straightening treatment ₩200,000–280,000, TOKIO treatment (6 sessions) ₩500,000 and Bunyadi treatment (6 sessions) ₩500,000.
Is there a product upgrade option?
Yes. Perms and treatments have a product upgrade option for ₩10,000–30,000.
