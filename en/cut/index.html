We talk through the look you want, your hair condition and your daily styling before we cut. Mina focuses on dandy, layered and design cuts, Kei on men's cuts, and Jun and Dagyeong on trend cuts.

We also offer cut + head spa menus. If you want volume too, see perms and volume straightening.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting an easy-to-manage dandy or layered cut
  • Students looking for student cut prices
  • Those who want a head spa with their cut
  • Anyone who needs an evening cut after work (until 10 pm on weekdays)

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a student haircut?
Boys' student cuts are ₩20,000 and girls' student cuts are ₩22,000–24,000.
Can I get a head spa with my haircut?
Yes. Men's cut + head spa is ₩50,000–60,000 and women's cut + head spa is ₩55,000–60,000.
Do you have designers who specialize in men's cuts?
Kei specializes in men's cuts and men's design perms, Hyemin in dandy styles and down perms, and Mina in dandy and layered cuts.
