FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book your date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-31-996-0472) or Instagram DM (@riahn_21), or book via Mamadne and Kakao Reservation.
Is parking available?
Yes, the building's basement parking lot is free.
Do you offer parking fee support at public parking?
Yes. Parking fee support is available if you use Sau Nooe public parking lot, 50 m from the salon.
What are your hours and closing days?
Mon–Fri 10:00–22:00 and Sat–Sun 10:00–20:00. Late weekday hours (until 10 pm) do not apply on public holidays, and we are closed on the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩20,000–60,000 (including cut + head spa), perms and straightening ₩30,000–250,000, color ₩50,000–180,000 (scalp covering ₩330,000–550,000), treatments and scalp care ₩30,000–500,000. Perms, color and treatments have a length surcharge of ₩10,000–50,000.
How long do services take?
Head spa (scalp care) takes 20–30 or 50–60 minutes, and the membership course (scalp care) 20–30 minutes. Please call us for the duration of other services.
Do you have student prices?
Yes. Student cuts are ₩20,000 for boys and ₩22,000–24,000 for girls.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your designer when you book. Each designer's specialties are on the Our Designers page.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes, pets are welcome on a leash.
Is the salon wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The entrance, restroom and seating are wheelchair accessible.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (Mon–Fri 10:00–22:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–20:00).
