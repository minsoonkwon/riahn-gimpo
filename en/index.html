About

Styles you will still love
tomorrow

We care more about how you feel in front of the mirror than about a pretty photo. Our goal is not just beautiful hair, but hair that stays beautiful.

We offer easy-to-manage styles, unhurried consultations and a warm space. At RIAHN Hair Gimpo City Hall we create signature styles you will be happy with tomorrow as well as today.

Open since 2013 with 18 styling chairs, we are at #402, Jeil Medical Center, 851 Gimpo-daero, Gimpo-si. On weekdays we stay open until 10 pm (except public holidays).

Good to know

  • Open late until 10 pm on weekdays (except public holidays)
  • Head spa and scalp care available
  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance, restroom and seating
  • Pets welcome on a leash
  • Free parking in the basement lot · parking fee support at Sau Nooe public parking lot (50 m)
