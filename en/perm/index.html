Home/Perms & Straightening

From layered perms to
volume straightening and men's down perms

We offer separate menus for women — basic, hippie and setting perms, volume straightening, root volume perms, root straightening and straightening + setting — and for men, including iron perms, hippie / spin perms and down perms.

Myeongji specializes in layered perms, setting perms and volume straightening, Hyemin in men's design perms and down perms, Hayun in iron perms and volume correction, and Jun in perm technique. For care alongside your perm, see treatment & scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a soft, face-framing layered or setting perm
  • Those who want smoother hair with volume or root straightening
  • Men looking for down perms, iron perms or design perms
  • Anyone who wants a quick bangs-only perm or straightening

FAQ

Perm FAQ

Do you have a men's perm menu?
Yes. Men's basic perm ₩80,000, hippie / spin perm ₩110,000, iron perm ₩120,000 and men's volume straightening ₩130,000. Down perms: men's cut + partial down perm ₩50,000–55,000, men's cut + full down perm ₩80,000–85,000.
How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening is ₩180,000–230,000, straightening + setting perm ₩200,000–250,000 and root straightening ₩150,000–200,000. Men's volume straightening is ₩130,000.
Can I get a perm on my bangs only?
Yes. A bangs basic perm is ₩30,000 and a bangs digital perm / straightening is ₩30,000–40,000.
